R reference: pipeline scripts

One entry per script in code/processing/R/, taken from the header block at the top of each file (Purpose, Reads, Writes, Depends, Notes), which is the authoritative description. Paths under “Reads” and “Writes” are relative to the data root (<repo>/data, or BSAT_DATA_DIR). main.R sources the scripts in numeric order; every script starts with rm(list = ls()) and communicates with the others only through files. How the scripts fit together is in Processing pipeline; how to bring in a new data vintage is in Updating raw data.

main.R

  • Purpose: run the full data-processing pipeline for the 2025-2034 period. Produces the four model-input files in processed/ (data_targets, data_exogenous, data_shocks, data_params_init) and the baseline package.
  • Run: Rscript main.R from code/processing/, or the run scripts. Sets its working directory to its own folder (through rstudioapi in RStudio, or the --file= argument under Rscript).
  • Data root: BSAT_DATA_DIR if set, else <repo>/data; stored in the option bsat.data_dir; interim/ and processed/ are created.
  • Order: 00 01 10 11 12 20 21 [22] [23] 24 30 40 50 60. Script 23 runs only when both large raw files are present; otherwise the shipped interim NTM results are reused, and without those the run stops.

00_load_packages.R

  • Purpose: check that every package the pipeline uses is installed and attach it; nothing is installed. Sourced first by main.R; later scripts clear objects but keep the attached packages. A missing or broken package stops the run with its name.
  • Reads: none.
  • Writes: none.
  • Depends: dplyr, tidyr, stringr, readr, readxl, openxlsx, zip, data.table, lfe, ggplot2 (from setup/environment.yml)
  • Notes: stringr, data.table and ggplot2 are loaded but no numbered script currently calls them; lfe is used only by 23.

01_check_region_commodity_coverage.R

  • Purpose: expand the region-commodity list of the USDA baseline to the full region x commodity grid and flag the pairs the baseline does not cover.
  • Reads: raw/region_commodity_list_2025.xlsx (sheet Sheet1: columns region, commodity).
  • Writes: interim/region_commodity_coverage.csv.
  • Depends: openxlsx, dplyr, tidyr, readr.
  • Notes: audit only; no later script reads the output.

10_read_baseline_data.R

  • Purpose: read the commodity sheets of the USDA International Long-Term Projections workbook and reshape them into one long table (commodity x region x year) holding the baseline variables.
  • Reads: raw/2025 International Long-Term Projections to 2034.xlsx (one sheet per commodity, named as in the commodity list); raw/region_commodity_list_2025.xlsx (sheet Sheet1: region order within each sheet).
  • Writes: interim/baseline_projections_long.csv.
  • Depends: openxlsx, dplyr, readr.
  • Notes: source vintage constant baseline_data <- "2025 International Long-Term Projections to 2034.xlsx". The sheet layout is hard-coded: each region block is 25 rows apart and 21 rows tall, starting at row 9 (Poultry) or row 7 (all other commodities); the number and meaning of the columns per commodity group (CL1 to CL8) is fixed in the script; the regions of a sheet are those of the region list, in order, followed by World. The string -- marks missing values; crop years 2025/26 keep the first year.

11_compute_feed_use_decomposition.R

  • Purpose: split each region’s total feed use of every feed crop across the three livestock products (beef, pork, poultry) in proportion to FAOSTAT livestock value-of-production shares.
  • Reads: raw/mapping.csv; raw/FAOSTAT_total_livestock_value_constant_IntlD.csv (2015 total livestock value, constant 2014-2016 I$); raw/FAOSTAT_each_livestock_value_constant_IntlD.csv (2015 value by livestock item, indigenous meat); interim/baseline_projections_long.csv (from 10).
  • Writes: interim/livestock_value_shares.xlsx; interim/feed_use_decomposition.xlsx.
  • Depends: dplyr, tidyr, readr, openxlsx.
  • Notes: Z_k = feed_only + ind_feed_other + feed_seed_waste for Barley, Corn, Sorghum, Soybeans, Soybean meal, Soybean oil and Wheat; Z_ik = Z_k x value share of livestock i. A missing value share is read as no demand (set to 0). FAOSTAT column Area Code (ISO3) and the five (indigenous) Item labels are matched by name; Myanmar is recoded MMR to BUR. Only group A regions and years from 2025 are kept.

12_generate_data_targets.R

  • Purpose: build data_targets.csv, the baseline levels the model is calibrated to, from the long baseline table and the feed-use decomposition.
  • Reads: interim/baseline_projections_long.csv (from 10); interim/feed_use_decomposition.xlsx (from 11); raw/mapping.csv; raw/baseline_units.csv (unit per commodity x attribute_description).
  • Writes: processed/data_targets.csv (overwritten afterwards by 50).
  • Depends: dplyr, tidyr, readr, readxl.
  • Notes: lst_obsv_yr <- 2025 is the first year of the analysis period; keep it equal to first_yr in 30. Commodities are renamed to model codes (Beef, SoyMeal, SoyOil, Soybean) and assigned to nests. Soybean-market consistency check: when a region has SoyOil or SoyMeal projections but no soybean market, SoyOil in BGD is reallocated to OAO and SoyOil and SoyMeal in ECO to ONA (see the long note in the script). All quantity attributes are rounded to whole units; yields and extraction rates are not (13 re-derives them).

20_compute_ag_expenditure_share.R

  • Purpose: compute the agricultural expenditure share (spending on the twelve modeled commodities as a percentage of real GDP) by region for 2025, and save the annual international price series built along the way.
  • Reads: processed/data_targets.csv (from 12, before harmonization: food and feed quantities); raw/2026-06-19 IMF Commodity Prices external-data.xlsx (sheet External: monthly, nine non-meat commodities); raw/2026-06-19 International Prices FAO Meat.xlsx (sheet data: monthly export prices, beef, pork, poultry); raw/2026-06-24 CPI_US.xlsx (sheet Sheet1: columns year, CPI_2017); raw/Global GDP Shares and GDP Growth Assumptions to 2035.xlsx (first sheet: USDA real GDP levels and growth); raw/mapping.csv.
  • Writes: interim/international_prices_annual.csv (marketing-year averages, native units, all years); interim/ag_expenditure_shares.csv.
  • Depends: openxlsx, dplyr, tidyr, readr.
  • Notes: IMF series are picked by the code in row 1 (imf_meta: PBARL, PWHEAMT, PCOTTIND, PRICENPQ, PMAIZMT, PSORG, PSOYB, PSMEA, PSOIL); dates are the YYYYMm labels in column 1. FAO meat prices are simple averages of fixed column positions (columns 3-5 beef, 6/8/9 pork, 7/10 poultry; dates MM/DD/YYYY in column 2). Prices are deflated to 2017 USD with CPI_2017 and averaged over USDA marketing years (start_mo); an incomplete last year uses the last 12 observed months. The GDP workbook is read by position (column 2 = 2025 level, columns 6-10 = growth 2025-2029, column 13 = 2026-35 average) and its region labels must match xwalk plus the OAO and ONA residual definitions. Only year 2025 is exported.

21_compute_land_cost_share.R

  • Purpose: compute the land cost share of value added (phi_f) for every region and crop from U.S. per-acre variable costs, relative yields and the USDA international TFP land cost shares.
  • Reads: raw/AgTFPInternational2023.xlsx (sheet Cost Shares, rows 3-182, columns 3 and 33: ISO3, 2021-2030); raw/variable_costs_2025/*to 2035*.xlsx (five USDA cost-of-production workbooks); raw/mapping.csv; interim/baseline_projections_long.csv (from 10: area and yield by region and crop).
  • Writes: interim/variable_costs_us_2025.csv (extracted U.S. per-acre variable costs, long); interim/region_commodity_land_cost_shares.csv.
  • Depends: openxlsx, dplyr, readr.
  • Notes: vintage constants: vc_pattern <- "to 2035" selects the cost workbooks in raw/variable_costs_2025/; the land-share column is renamed from its header 2021-2030. The EUR land share is hard-coded at 0.113. Cost workbooks are parsed by row label: Table ... U.S. <crop>, an Item header row with the crop years, Variable costs of production and the following Per acre row. Land rents solve w * sum_k s_rk / (H_rk + w) = land share with uniroot on [0, 1000]; shares are winsorized at the 5th and 95th percentiles within each year. Years from 2025 are exported. The derivation is in data/land cost share.md.

22_compute_tariff_ave.R

  • Purpose: compute region-commodity ad valorem import tariffs (simple average of WITS MFN tariffs, latest reported year per product) and a zero export-tax scaffold.
  • Reads: raw/trade_barriers/tariff_ave_WITS_2023.csv (WITS TRAINS export: Reporter, Product, DutyType, Simple Average, Tariff Year); raw/trade_barriers/country_code_WITS.xlsx (columns country, ISO3, Reporter); raw/mapping.csv.
  • Writes: interim/tariff_ave_region.csv; interim/export_taxes_comm_reg.csv.
  • Depends: openxlsx, dplyr, tidyr, readr.
  • Notes: HS codes are mapped to commodities by fixed 4- and 6-digit lists (0201, 0202, 0207, 1001, 1003, 1005, 1006, 1007, 1201, 1507, 2304, 5201-5203, …). Missing tariffs are set to 0 (with the current data: all of Iraq, Saudi Arabia pork, Pakistan soybean oil). The EU is one reporter (EUN). Export taxes are not observed and are 0 everywhere. The year column of the tariff output is the string 2023 (or last observed).

23_compute_NTM_ave.R

  • Purpose: estimate a model of non-tariff-measure ad valorem equivalents (AVE) on tariffs, GDP per capita and agricultural imports with region x product fixed effects, impute missing AVEs, and aggregate them into GDP-weighted region-commodity import NTMs; export NTMs are a zero scaffold.
  • Reads: raw/trade_barriers/ave_hs_importer.csv (World Bank NTM AVEs: importer, hs2code, ave1C, ave2C); raw/trade_barriers/BACI_HS92_Y2014_V202401b.csv (CEPII bilateral flows 2014: t, i, j, k, v, q; large, not in the repository); raw/trade_barriers/country_codes_V202401b.csv (BACI numeric code to country_iso3); raw/trade_barriers/ag_hs_products.csv (agricultural HS product list, column Product); raw/trade_barriers/ag_tariff_USA_2014.xls (sheet Applied_MFN, column E from row 6: HS6 codes); raw/trade_barriers/tariff_ave_2014_WITS.csv (WITS 2014 tariff AVEs; large, not in the repository); raw/trade_barriers/country_code_WITS.xlsx (Reporter to ISO3); raw/trade_barriers/country_region_WB.xlsx (sheet List of economies, rows 1-219, columns 2-3: Code, Region); raw/current_GDPpc_2014_WB.csv and raw/current_GDP_2014_WB.csv (World Bank, column 2014 [YR2014]); raw/current_GDPpc_IMF.xls and raw/current_GDP_IMF.xls (IMF WEO, Taiwan only); raw/mapping.csv.
  • Writes: interim/model_estimates.RData (rxp_fe and the four beta coefficients); interim/NTM_regression_results.csv (coefficients, standard errors and fit statistics); interim/NTM_regression_table.tex (the same results as a LaTeX table for the paper); interim/NTM_ave_imputed.RData (NTM_ave_imputed: country x commodity actual and imputed AVE); interim/NTM_IMP_ave_region.csv; interim/NTM_EXP_ave_region.csv.
  • Depends: dplyr, tidyr, readr, readxl, openxlsx, lfe.
  • Notes: sourced by main.R only when both large raw files are present; otherwise the two interim CSVs are reused. Regression: ave ~ AVE_Tariff + AVE_Tariff_ag + log_GDPpc + log_total_imp_ag | region_geo:product_id (felm). Negative imputations are set to 0; gaps for Barley, Sorghum, Soybeans and Wheat take the country’s Corn, Rice and Cotton average; Iraq takes its geographic-region average. EU members plus GBR are pooled as EUN; Myanmar MMR to BUR; Taiwan is BACI code 490. World Bank regions are merged into Euro-MENA and Asia-Pacific for the fixed effects. Reference year of all inputs is 2014. Diagnostics: 25. The regression is described in data/impute_ave_NTM.R.md.

24_generate_data_exogenous.R

  • Purpose: assemble data_exogenous.csv: calibrated parameters, 2025 international prices, population and GDP per capita, unit price normalizations, livestock inventory and slaughter, and the interim results of 20 to 23.
  • Reads: raw/exogenous_data.csv (calibrated elasticities and exogenous parameters, model schema); raw/mapping.csv; raw/population_WB_2025.xls (sheet Data, header on row 4, one column per year); raw/population_IMF_WEO_2025.csv (IMF WEO, Taiwan only); raw/Current_GDP_WB_2025.xls (sheet Data, header on row 4); raw/Current_GDP_IMF_WEO_2025.csv (Taiwan only); raw/FAOSTAT_Livestock_Inventory_2024.csv and raw/FAOSTAT_Slaughtered Livestock_2024.csv (Item, Year, Value; Area Code (ISO3)); interim/international_prices_annual.csv and interim/ag_expenditure_shares.csv (from 20); interim/region_commodity_land_cost_shares.csv (from 21); interim/tariff_ave_region.csv and interim/export_taxes_comm_reg.csv (from 22); interim/NTM_IMP_ave_region.csv and interim/NTM_EXP_ave_region.csv (from 23, or the shipped interim copy).
  • Writes: processed/data_exogenous.csv.
  • Depends: dplyr, tidyr, readr, readxl.
  • Notes: year constants are hard-coded: prices, population and GDP use 2025; livestock inventory and slaughter use FAOSTAT Year == 2024. Population is in 1,000 persons, GDP per capita in current USD. Prices become million 2017 USD per 1,000 native units (cotton: 1,000 480-lb bales). IMF WEO files supply Taiwan only (COUNTRY == "Taiwan Province of China"; GDP in billions, population in millions). FAOSTAT Item labels are matched by name. Land Unavailability is renamed with a trailing space to match the model. input_path_units (baseline_units.csv) is declared but not read.

25_compare_actual_imputed_NTM.R

  • Purpose: diagnostics for the NTM imputation of 23: a scatter plot of actual versus imputed AVE with a fitted line, and Excel tables of the observations that could or could not be imputed.
  • Reads: interim/NTM_ave_imputed.RData (object NTM_ave_imputed, written by 23).
  • Writes: outputs/diagnostics/actual_vs_imputed_AVE.pdf; outputs/diagnostics/NTM_AVE_diagnostics.xlsx.
  • Depends: dplyr, openxlsx; base graphics.
  • Notes: not sourced by main.R. Run it manually after 23 has written interim/NTM_ave_imputed.RData (which needs the two large raw files): from code/processing, source("R/00_load_packages.R") and then source("R/25_compare_actual_imputed_NTM.R"); set options(bsat.data_dir = ...) first if the data live elsewhere. The outputs/diagnostics folder is created if needed.

30_generate_data_shocks.R

  • Purpose: generate data_shocks.csv, a zero-filled scaffold of every shock attribute for each region, commodity and year of the analysis period. Levels live in data_exogenous.csv; this file holds the deviations.
  • Reads: raw/mapping.csv (the 42 model regions).
  • Writes: processed/data_shocks.csv.
  • Depends: dplyr, tidyr, readr, tibble.
  • Notes: first_yr <- 2025 and last_yr <- 2034 define the horizon; keep first_yr equal to lst_obsv_yr in 12. Ten shock attributes: four trade barriers (all commodities), TFP value added and land productivity (crops), TFP feed use (livestock), TFP intermediates (SoyMeal, SoyOil), and two region-level shocks (ag expenditure share, land supply). Two attribute names contain spaces. Editing these values is how counterfactual scenarios are defined.

40_generate_data_params_init.R

  • Purpose: generate data_params_init.csv, the initial values of the seven commodity-level substitution and transformation elasticities.
  • Reads: none.
  • Writes: processed/data_params_init.csv.
  • Depends: dplyr, readr.
  • Notes: values are hard-coded: sigma_d 3.8 (12 commodities), sigma_f 1.1 (7 crops), sigma_c 5 (SoyMeal, SoyOil), sigma_l 0.25, sigma_z 0.5 and sigma_w 3.8 (livestock), omega_s 5 (12 commodities); 42 rows with attribute type C.

50_harmonize_targets.R

  • Purpose: apply to the rounded targets of 12 the same three adjustments the model would make on its own, in the model’s order, so that the model finds no residual to repair and the neutral run reproduces every target exactly.
  • Reads: processed/data_targets.csv (written by 12).
  • Writes: processed/data_targets.csv (overwritten, harmonized); interim/harmonization_log.csv (columns region, year, commodity, check (consumption or market), residual, adjusted (the attribute changed), delta, status).
  • Depends: dplyr, tidyr, readr.
  • Notes: COTTON_KG_PER_BALE <- 217.728111162486 is the model’s bale-to-kg factor. Three adjustments, all logged, in the model’s order. (a) Consumption: a residual TC - (FOOD + LIV + OTR + CRSH) within plus or minus 2 is added to the largest of those four components, the first maximum in that order; when the largest is feed to livestock or other feed, the rows are left alone and the residual is left to the model (status left to the model: feed component), because the model repairs it in its internal feed totals but reports feed consumption from the unrepaired target (df_DCFEED1 in model_core.jl). (b) Market: a residual of BS + Q + M - Loss = TC + X + ES within plus or minus 2 is subtracted from the largest of beginning stocks, production and imports, the first maximum in that order. (c) Derived series: yields as production over area (cotton in kg per hectare) and extraction rates as 100 times production over soybean crushing, both computed from the adjusted values. Residuals outside plus or minus 2 are not adjusted; they are logged with a status containing outside the model's ±2 window and raise a warning(), and the model refuses that year.

60_build_input_package.R

  • Purpose: bundle the four processed CSV files and a METADATA.csv into the standard model input package (a zip with an Input/ folder) specified in Package and file format. The shocks file holds neutral values, so the package is the baseline package.
  • Reads: processed/data_params_init.csv; processed/data_exogenous.csv; processed/data_targets.csv; processed/data_shocks.csv.
  • Writes: packages/baseline_<first year>_Input.zip (first year = smallest year in data_targets.csv).
  • Depends: readr, dplyr, zip, tibble.
  • Notes: METADATA.csv records Author, Description, Comments and the build timestamp (Pipeline build). An existing zip with the same name is replaced.
